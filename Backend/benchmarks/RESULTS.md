## 1. Quick Start: Reproducing the Benchmarks

All benchmarks are deterministic and runnable with a single CLI command from the repository root:

```bash
# Run all 4 evaluation suites in quick mode (terminal summary + JSON artifact)
cd Backend
python -m benchmarks.runner --suite all --quick

# Run an individual suite
python -m benchmarks.runner --suite rag
python -m benchmarks.runner --suite cat
python -m benchmarks.runner --suite bkt
python -m benchmarks.runner --suite policy
```

Benchmark artifacts are written directly to `Backend/benchmarks/results/latest_benchmark.json`.

## 4. Dimension 2: 1PL Rasch Adaptive CAT Psychometric Ability Recovery

### Experimental Setup
* **Model:** Rasch 1-Parameter Logistic (1PL) Item Response Theory:
  $$P(Y_{ij} = 1 \mid \theta_i, b_j) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-(\theta_i - b_j)}}$$
* **Item Selection Rule:** Maximum Fisher Information at current estimate $\hat{\theta}$:
  $$I_j(\hat{\theta}) = P_j(\hat{\theta})(1 - P_j(\hat{\theta}))$$
* **Synthetic Population:** $N = 150$ simulated examinees across 5 ability strata:
  $$\theta_{\text{true}} \in \{-2.0, -1.0, 0.0, +1.0, +2.0\}$$
* **Stopping Rule:** Standard Error $\text{SE}(\hat{\theta}) \le 0.35$ or Maximum Test Length $N_{\max} = 10$.
* **Initial Prior:** $\hat{\theta}_0 = 0.0, \text{SE}_0 = 1.0$.

### Quantitative Results

| Policy | Mean Abs Error (MAE) | Root Mean Sq Error (RMSE) | Bias | Mean Items Administered | Mean Final SE |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ADAPT-01 (Adaptive Fisher Info)** | **0.5202** | **0.6173** | **+0.0038** | **10.0** | **0.5699** |
| **Fixed Difficulty ($b = 0.0$)** | 0.6183 | 0.7126 | +0.0128 | 10.0 | 0.5750 |
| **Random Item Selection** | 0.5803 | 0.7106 | +0.1105 | 9.92 | 0.6338 |

### Ability Stratum Breakdown (Adaptive CAT)

| True Ability Stratum | Sample Size | Mean Absolute Error (MAE) | Mean Items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\theta = -2.0$ (Novice) | 30 | 0.7450 | 10.0 |
| $\theta = -1.0$ (Developing) | 30 | 0.4112 | 10.0 |
| $\theta = 0.0$ (Average) | 30 | **0.3354** | 10.0 |
| $\theta = +1.0$ (Proficient) | 30 | 0.5310 | 10.0 |
| $\theta = +2.0$ (Advanced) | 30 | 0.5785 | 10.0 |

**Scientific Interpretation:**
ADAPT-01 achieves a **13.13% reduction in RMSE** compared to random testing under equivalent 10-item test constraints. Ability recovery is sharpest near the center of the item pool ($\theta = 0.0$, $\text{MAE} = 0.3354$), reflecting optimal item information targeting.

## 5. Dimension 3: Corbett & Anderson Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) Calibration

### Experimental Setup
* **Model:** Classic 4-parameter BKT (Corbett & Anderson 1994):
  - Initial Prior: $P(L_0) = 0.20$
  - Transit Probability: $P(T) = 0.15$
  - Slip Rate: $P(S) = 0.10$
  - Guess Rate: $P(G) = 0.20$
* **Evaluation Corpus:** 50 synthetic multi-turn learner interaction traces totaling 400 observation steps.
* **Evaluation Objectives:**
  - *Brier Score:* Mean squared error between predicted next-step performance $\hat{p}_t$ and binary outcome $y_t \in \{0, 1\}$.
  - *Log Loss (Cross-Entropy):* Information-theoretic penalty for overconfidence.
  - *AUC-ROC:* Capacity to discriminate between students who understand vs do not.
  - *Expected Calibration Error (ECE):* Weighted average deviation across 10 confidence bins:
    $$\text{ECE} = \sum_{m=1}^{10} \frac{|B_m|}{N} \left| \text{acc}(B_m) - \text{conf}(B_m) \right|$$

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Measured Value | Reference Baseline | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brier Score** | **0.1834** | 0.2500 (Uncalibrated Coin Flip) | **26.64% error reduction** over uncalibrated baseline |
| **Log Loss** | **0.5400** | 0.6931 ($\ln 2$) | Substantial information gain |
| **AUC-ROC** | **0.7534** | 0.5000 (Random Chance) | Strong discriminatory separation |
| **Expected Calibration Error (ECE)** | **0.0643** | > 0.1500 (Overconfident Models) | Exceptional calibration (<7% mean gap) |

### 10-Bin Calibration Reliability Diagram

```text
Confidence Bin  | Samples | Predicted Conf | Empirical Accuracy | Calibration Gap
────────────────┼─────────┼────────────────┼────────────────────┼────────────────
[0.70 - 0.80)   |   39    |     78.91%     |       92.31%       |     13.40%
[0.80 - 0.90)   |  115    |     88.25%     |       92.17%       |      3.92%
Weighted ECE: 0.0643 (6.43% overall calibration error)
```

**Scientific Interpretation:**
Knowledge tracing probabilities feed downstream pedagogical recommendations. A low ECE (0.0643) confirms that when Shiro predicts an 88% probability of success, the learner actually succeeds ~92% of the time, avoiding premature graduation or redundant review.

## 6. Dimension 4: REC-01 Multi-Constraint Policy Simulation

### Experimental Setup
* **Engine:** REC-01 Pruned Beam Search ($K = 8$) sequence optimizer.
* **Constraint Set:**
  1. *Time Budget:* Hard cap on cumulative duration (15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, 90m).
  2. *Cognitive Pacing:* Pedagogical ordering: $\text{Warmup} \prec \text{Core} \prec \text{Consolidation}$.
  3. *Prerequisite Knowledge Graph:* Concepts cannot be assigned before prerequisites achieve $P(L) \ge 0.50$.
  4. *Modality Diversity:* Penalizes consecutive identical activities.
* **Baselines Benchmarked:**
  1. *Random:* Uninformed activity assignment.
  2. *BKT-Only:* Greedy selection prioritizing minimum mastery $1 - P(L)$.
  3. *Retention-Only:* Greedy selection prioritizing maximum memory decay $1 - R(t)$.
  4. *Exam-Priority:* Weighted strictly toward upcoming exam blueprint topics.
  5. *Greedy ROI:* Heuristic picking the highest immediate $\Delta \text{Mastery} + \Delta \text{Retention} / t$ with zero lookahead.
  6. *REC-01 (Shiro):* Constrained Pruned Beam Search.

### Benchmark Results (Standard 45-Minute Session)

| Policy | Mean Utility | Retention Gain ($\Delta R$) | Mastery Gain ($\Delta L$) | Budget Utilization | Pacing Adherence | Prereq Violations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Random Assignment** | 0.572 | 0.639 | 0.611 | 94.2% | 0.0% | 1.00 |
| **BKT-Only** | 1.001 | 0.506 | **1.869** | 95.6% | **100.0%** | 0.60 |
| **Retention-Only** | 1.052 | **2.464** | 0.326 | 88.9% | **100.0%** | 1.60 |
| **Exam-Priority** | 0.805 | 1.715 | 0.364 | 88.9% | **100.0%** | **0.00** |
| **Greedy ROI** | **1.258** | 2.091 | 1.068 | 94.7% | 40.0% | 1.20 |
| **REC-01 (Shiro)** | **1.192** | 1.270 | 1.333 | 93.3% | **100.0%** | 0.80 |

### Multi-Budget Utility Curve

| Policy | 15m Budget | 30m Budget | 45m Budget | 60m Budget | 90m Budget |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Random** | 0.294 | 0.532 | 0.572 | 0.668 | 0.812 |
| **BKT-Only** | 0.288 | 0.620 | 1.001 | 0.947 | 1.350 |

| **REC-01 (Shiro)** | **0.590** | **0.950** | 1.192 | 1.240 | 1.580 |

### Honest Scientific Trade-Off Analysis

1. **REC-01 vs Random (+108.39% Gain):**
   Under a standard 45-minute budget, REC-01 more than doubles the aggregate learning utility (+108.39%) compared to unguided student choices, eliminating zero-pacing sequences.

2. **The REC-01 vs Greedy ROI Trade-Off (-5.25% Raw Utility, +60% Pacing Adherence):**
   * Raw utility for Greedy ROI (1.258) is slightly higher than REC-01 (1.192) by 5.25%.
   * **Why does this happen?** Greedy ROI aggressively picks flashcards and quizzes with high immediate score-per-minute regardless of sequence logic. In doing so, it violates cognitive pacing 60% of the time (e.g., throwing a student directly into an intense 15-minute diagnostic quiz before activating working memory with a concept warmup).
   * **REC-01 enforces hard pedagogical constraints:** It guarantees 100% adherence to the $\text{Warmup} \prec \text{Core} \prec \text{Consolidation}$ sequence, accepting a modest 5% raw utility cost to ensure pedagogical soundness and lower cognitive fatigue.

## 7. Limitations & Future Work (Version 4.0 Scope)

1. **Controlled Synthetic Cohorts:** Current evaluations use simulated student traces governed by 1PL Rasch and Corbett-Anderson probabilistic dynamics. Future work (v4.0) will incorporate de-identified longitudinal classroom interaction logs via LTI 1.3 integrations.
2. **Item Parameter Calibration:** Difficulties are currently calibrated across synthetic batches. v4.0 will implement continuous Marginal Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MMLE) via Expectation-Maximization in Celery workers.
3. **Institutional Tenancy:** Multi-tenant org hierarchies, SCIM provisioning, and SAML/OIDC SSO are scheduled for the Enterprise v4.0 release.
